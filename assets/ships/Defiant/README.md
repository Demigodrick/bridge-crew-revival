# Defiant

Select **DEFIANT** in the briefing room (scroll the ship box down), or set `[Ships] PlayerShip = Defiant`.
It uses the game's Aegis bridge and systems, with these changes:

- Pulse cannons: each fire command sends a short gold burst from a pair of cannons in the nacelle roots, upper and lower pairs in turn, within 25 degrees of straight ahead. A full phaser bank gives eight bursts. The second cannon's pulses are for show, so damage and energy are one burst's.
- Torpedoes: a single shot fires both forward tubes, each using its own torpedo. With `[Ships] QuantumTorpedoes` on (the default), they are quantum torpedoes, as on the Sovereign.
- Handling: it turns three times as fast and accelerates 1.6 times as fast. Top speed, hull and shields are the Aegis's.

The ship's name and registry are painted on the dorsal ring and the bow.
